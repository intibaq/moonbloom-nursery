Privacy Policy

Moonbloom Nursery · Last updated 26 November 2026

Moonbloom Nursery ("the game") is published on the App Store by aligniqs, a trading name of INTIBAQ COMPANY ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

In short: we have no accounts and no servers, and we never see your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google when you choose to watch an optional reward video, and to Apple when you make a purchase.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your game progress: plants, rooms, coins, gems, settings and the record of your purchases. It is saved only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted if you delete the game.
  • A local activity log: which screens and buttons were used and when, with no names or personal details. It never leaves your device.

2. Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short video for a reward (energy, coins or gems). The game never shows an ad you did not ask for. These videos come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When the game loads or shows a video, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below) and an identifier for this app on your device;
  • your IP address, from which a general location (such as country or city) can be estimated;
  • information about the ads: which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped;
  • diagnostics such as crash and performance information from the ad software.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and improve its services, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices about ads

  • Tracking (Apple): the first time you choose to watch a video, iPhone asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, Google cannot use the advertising identifier, and ads are less personalised. The game plays exactly the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland): where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks you before any ad data is used. You can change your choice later in the game's Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Not watching: you never have to watch an ad. Everything in the game can be reached without them.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple tells the game only which item was bought and a transaction number, which the game keeps on your device so a purchase is never given twice and can be restored. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • We do not run our own analytics, and we do not sell or share information with anyone else.
  • We do not have accounts, chat or any way for players to contact each other.

6. Children

The game is made for a general and adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the reward videos and want information removed, contact us and we will help you reach Google.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Your game data can be removed at any time by deleting the game. For data held by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls, or contact us and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
