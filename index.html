Moonbloom Nursery

Breed flowers, restore a home · for iPhone

Your grandmother Ella has left you her old plant nursery. Plant seeds, merge them into seedlings, buds and full blooms, and hand them to the neighbours waiting for them. Every order brings back a piece of the house, room by room, until you find the flower Ella never stopped looking for: the Moonbloom.

  • Grow and merge seven kinds of flowers and herbs.
  • Breed new colours, scents and petal shapes, and see the odds before you breed.
  • Restore five rooms, from the Entrance to the Moon Room.
  • Over a hundred story orders from a warm cast of neighbours.
  • No timers that punish you, and no ads you didn't ask for.
