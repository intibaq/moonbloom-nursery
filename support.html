Help & Support

Moonbloom Nursery
Write to us: sowilems@hotmail.com
Please include your iPhone model, the game's version (Settings, at the bottom) and what happened. We usually answer within two working days.

I bought something but didn't get it

Close the game fully and open it again: purchases that were interrupted are delivered when the game starts. If it is still missing, open the Store and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, write to us with the date of the purchase from your Apple receipt email.

How do I restore the starter pack or the nursery pass on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the Store and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. A pass restores with the days it has left. Gem packs are used up when bought and cannot be restored.

Is my progress saved online?

No. Your nursery is saved on your phone only, so it works with no internet. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted too.

I started over. Did I lose my purchases?

No. Starting over in Settings restarts the story and the garden, but keeps your gems, the pass and everything you bought in the Store.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

The reward video didn't give me anything

The reward comes only when the video is watched to the end. Sometimes no video is available, especially without an internet connection; try again in a minute. Each kind of video has a daily limit.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Moonbloom Nursery. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can also open the game's Settings › Ad privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I turn off sound, vibration or motion?

Yes, in the game's Settings (the gear). The game also follows your iPhone's Reduce Motion setting and supports VoiceOver.
